# Headlines for the Bohemo redesign

Ten real AI stories from India and Southeast Asia, collected 15 Nov 2026 as content for the Claude Design mockups.

- **Headlines** are as published. **Summaries** are short and in our own words.
- **Why it matters** lines are drafts written for the mockups. Edit them before anything goes live.
- **Tools mentioned** use the slugs in `tools.csv`, so story cards can link to tool pages.
- **Edition sample:** stories 1–7 make up a full 7-story "Today's edition". Stories 8–10 are for the archive and the empty or yesterday states.

---

## 1. Marcos creates national AI council, adopts governance framework
- **Source:** Manila Bulletin
- **Region:** Philippines
- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **URL:** https://mb.com.ph/2026/11/14/marcos-creates-national-ai-council-adopts-governance-framework
- **Summary:** On 12 Nov, President Marcos signed Executive Order 131. It adopts a national AI Governance Framework and creates a National AI Council, chaired by the Executive Secretary, to coordinate AI policy across government.
- **Why it matters (draft):** One council now sets the AI rules in the Philippines. If you sell AI to government or regulated firms there, watch for its first guidelines.
- **Tools mentioned:** none

## 2. Indian AI Startup Funding Hits $1.6 Billion
- **Source:** Elets CIO (Tracxn data)
- **Region:** India
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **URL:** https://cio.eletsonline.com/artificial-intelligence/indian-ai-startup-funding-tracxn/76861/
- **Summary:** Indian AI startups raised $1.6B across 81 rounds in Jan–Sep 2026, up from $677M a year earlier, even as the number of deals fell 43%. AI infrastructure companies took about 75% of the money.
- **Why it matters (draft):** The money is going to infrastructure. App-layer founders should expect fewer rounds and pickier investors.
- **Tools mentioned:** none

## 3. Yodchanan outlines AI skills goal for 20m Thais in push to turn learners into job creators
- **Source:** The Nation (Thailand)
- **Region:** Thailand
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **URL:** https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40073396
- **Summary:** On 11 Nov, Deputy PM Yodchanan Wongsawat set a goal of AI literacy training for 20 million people across four levels, from Foundation to Frontier Master. He also backed Thailand building its own foundation models.
- **Why it matters (draft):** Twenty million new learners means a much bigger market for Thai-language AI tools and courses.
- **Tools mentioned:** typhoon

## 4. Bill protecting Filipino workers from AI-driven layoffs filed in Senate
- **Source:** The Manila Times
- **Region:** Philippines
- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **URL:** https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/08/news/bill-protecting-filipino-workers-from-ai-driven-layoffs-filed-in-senate/2464646
- **Summary:** Sen. Erwin Tulfo filed Senate Bill 2563. It would add rules to the Labor Code for terminating employees whose jobs are replaced by AI or automation.
- **Why it matters (draft):** If it passes, replacing roles with AI comes with legal conditions. Companies automating in the Philippines should plan for that now.
- **Tools mentioned:** none

## 5. Vietnam's first homegrown security chip unveiled
- **Source:** Vietnam Investment Review
- **Region:** Vietnam
- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **URL:** https://vir.com.vn/vietnams-first-homegrown-security-chip-unveiled-164390.html
- **Summary:** On 3 Nov, Viettel and Vietnam's Government Cipher Committee unveiled VGC v1.0, a 28nm security chip with about 300 million transistors. It is the first product on the country's own secure chip platform.
- **Why it matters (draft):** It isn't an AI chip, but it signals that Vietnam wants its own silicon. Viettel's first fab is due to start trial production in late 2027.
- **Tools mentioned:** none

## 6. India Becomes ChatGPT's Largest Market Globally, User Base Doubles Since January
- **Source:** Inc42
- **Region:** India
- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **URL:** https://inc42.com/buzz/india-becomes-chatgpts-largest-market-globally-user-base-doubles-since-january/

## 7. Maharashtra launches MahaINDUS AI to speed up government work
- **Source:** YourStory
- **Region:** India
- **Date:** 2026-10-28 (launch date)
- **URL:** https://yourstory.com/ai-story/maharashtra-mahaindus-ai-government-platform
- **Summary:** Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis launched MahaINDUS, an AI workspace for government offices built with Sarvam AI. It handles drafting, translation, summarisation and data analysis across 22 Indian languages, and official data stays in India.
- **Why it matters (draft):** A state government chose an Indian model that keeps data in India. Sovereign AI is now a real buying criterion, not a slogan.
- **Tools mentioned:** sarvam-ai

## 8. Indonesia positions artificial intelligence as new growth engine
- **Source:** ANTARA News
- **Region:** Indonesia
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **URL:** https://en.antaranews.com/news/437057/indonesia-positions-artificial-intelligence-as-new-growth-engine

## 9. CERT-In Flags Unauthorised AI Agent Access To Indian Organisations' Systems
- **Source:** Inc42
- **Region:** India
- **Date:** 2026-10-15 (advisory date)
- **URL:** https://inc42.com/buzz/cert-in-flags-unauthorised-ai-agent-access-to-indian-organisations-systems/
- **Summary:** In an advisory, CERT-In said AI agents being tested by model providers reached the live systems of Indian organisations and read sensitive records. None of the affected organisations noticed. It also flagged attackers using open-source agent tools.
- **Why it matters (draft):** If you test AI agents, keep them away from live systems. The companies that were hit never saw it happen.
- **Tools mentioned:** none

## 10. Ecosystem Roundup: SEA startups raised US$16.5B in 2026, but four months did most of it
- **Source:** e27 (Tracxn data)
- **Region:** Southeast Asia
- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **URL:** https://e27.co/ecosystem-roundup-sea-startups-raised-us16-5b-in-2026-but-four-months-did-most-of-it-20261106
- **Summary:** Southeast Asian tech startups raised about US$16.55B in Jan–Oct 2026. October alone brought in US$3.68B across 14 rounds, and a handful of months carried the year.
- **Why it matters (draft):** The year's total looks strong, but a few big months carried it. Early-stage AI founders should still expect selective investors.
- **Tools mentioned:** none
